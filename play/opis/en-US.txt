PUN OPIS (2131/4000):
On the road in France and need fuel, a coffee, a restroom or a place to rest? Autoroutes Info shows what's ahead on every French autoroute – exit by exit, sorted by kilometre, in your direction of travel.

Pick a motorway and your direction, and you get:
• every exit and interchange with its kilometre
• service areas and rest areas on your side of the road
• fuel stations with brand logos, restaurants and facilities (WC, showers, shop, hotel)
• EV chargers and truck parking spaces
• phone number and website where available
• national average price of petrol (SP95), diesel and LPG, updated weekly

DRIVING MODE
Start Driving mode and the app finds the motorway you are on, the kilometre (as on the roadside markers) and your direction, and shows the next exits, service areas and fuel stations ahead. When you change motorway, the list follows you. Stop it with one tap or by closing the app.

TRAFFIC NEWS
Accidents, queues, closures and roadworks from Bison Futé on the state-run network, with direction. With notifications on, you get alerts for the motorway you selected or the one you are driving on. Toll motorways (Vinci, APRR, Sanef…) are not included yet.

COVERAGE
50 motorways, from the A1 Paris–Lille and A6 Paris–Lyon to the A7 Autoroute du Soleil, the A10, the A9 and the A8 to the Riviera.

WORKS OFFLINE
All motorway data is built into the app, so the list works without signal. Only traffic news and fuel prices need a connection.

MADE FOR DRIVERS
• one tap opens navigation to the place
• share any service area or exit
• filters: fuel, food, parking, exits, EV charging
• no account, no sign-up; your location stays on your phone
• one-time purchase to remove ads
• 18 languages

DATA SOURCES
Motorway data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Official kilometres: Wikipedia. Traffic: Bison Futé (Licence Ouverte). Fuel prices: weekly national average (fuel-prices.eu, EU Weekly Oil Bulletin), not the price at a specific station.
